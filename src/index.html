Mod Checker

Check if all your mods are ready for a new Minecraft version.

  1. Add your mods. Drag your .jar files onto the window, or click Import mods… / Import folder….
  2. Pick your loader and version. Choose the loader (Fabric, Forge, NeoForge or Quilt) and the Minecraft version you want, like 26.3.
  3. Read the colors. Green = ready, Yellow = has an update for that version, Red = no build for that version yet.
  4. Red mods: click Find alternatives to see similar mods that already work on your version.
  5. Test launch. Click ▶ Test launch to see what would happen if you started the game, and how to fix anything that would break.
  6. Update. Click Update on a yellow mod. Your old jar is saved in a .old-mods folder.
  7. Optional: paste a free CurseForge API key under the settings to also check CurseForge mods. Modrinth works without one.
